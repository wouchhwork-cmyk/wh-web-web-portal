Business settings

These are set once, here, and everything else reads them. Nothing keeps its own copy — so changing one here changes it everywhere at once, rather than leaving two parts of the product quietly disagreeing.

Every change is recorded and kept as a version, because a reward granted last month was decided against the settings in force last month.

What “today” and “6pm” mean for this business. It decides when “once a day” resets and when a happy hour opens, so a reward rule with a cap or a time window needs it. Pick where the business actually operates — nothing guesses this for you.
What money means here. Only needed if a reward ever grants money — a points-only programme never uses it, so leaving it unset is a real answer rather than an unfinished one.

What customers see

Everything in this panel is written for people outside the business. It is what goes into a message sent on your behalf and anywhere else a customer meets you — so write it the way you would say it to them, not the way it appears on paperwork.

Your business is registered as one thing and known as another. This is the known one. Leave it empty and messages fall back to the registered name.
Who you are and what you are known for, in your own words. Up to 5,000 characters — 0 used.
Anywhere you want customers to go: ordering, directions, your app, whatever is useful. The name is what they read — so it is a name, not the address again. Pick a suggestion or type your own.
How a customer reaches a person. Add more than one if you need to — by region, or orders separately from support. A phone number needs its country code, because the person reading it cannot guess the country.
