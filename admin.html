Meta API rate limits

Meta runs two separate meters and the token decides which applies. The app pool is one allowance for the whole developer app — roughly 200 × daily active users per hour — and it is drained by businesses connecting, not by the inbox. The business pools are per business per product and carry everything else.

Every figure Meta gives is a percentage of an allowance it never states, so “used” is how close a pool is to being refused, and “calls” is our own count. A pool showing unknown is one Meta sent no header for — that is not the same as zero.
