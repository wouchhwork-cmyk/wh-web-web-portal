Roles

A role is a level plus a set of permissions. You can only create roles below your own level, and you can only give a role permissions you hold yourself — so nobody can build a way around their own limits.

Built-in roles cannot be edited. If agent is not quite right for your business, make your own role instead — changing a built-in one would change it for everybody who already has it.
